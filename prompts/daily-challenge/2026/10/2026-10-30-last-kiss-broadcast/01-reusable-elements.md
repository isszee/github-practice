# Reusable Elements

## Character

- Adult woman, elegant and wistful presence
- Petite-to-average height with a model-slender hourglass silhouette
- Porcelain-white skin with pale peach undertones and fine natural texture
- Large light-brown eyes, rose-full lips
- Short light chestnut-brown French bob with wispy fringe
- Intelligent, melancholic, quietly composed expression

## Environment

- Abandoned 1980s television broadcast control room
- Stacked CRT monitors with blue static
- Fading red ON AIR lamp
- Old broadcast microphone
- Tangled coaxial cables, dust motes, cracked screens, layered control panels
- Midnight emergency-power atmosphere

## Wardrobe and materials

- Vintage ivory silk tea-length dress
- Delicate lace with subtle aging, dust, and minor imperfection
- Thin silver choker
- Soft silk sheen contrasted with lace absorbing the light

## Narrative devices

- Reflection behaves differently from the real subject
- A lost lover appears only as a blurred memory or monitor reflection
- The final broadcast becomes a metaphor for love becoming legend
- Supernatural details remain tasteful and non-graphic

## Lighting and color

- Cold cyan-blue CRT glow as the environmental key
- Warm 2700K tungsten emergency lamp as the counter-source
- Blue rim light around hair and shoulders
- Soft warm fill on the face
- Deep ink-black shadows and controlled chiaroscuro
- Palette: muted ivory, midnight blue, cyan, charcoal black, faded rose, emergency red

## Camera and finish

- Full-frame camera look
- 50mm lens, f/1.4
- Eye-level camera, intimate three-quarter angle
- Shallow depth of field
- Sharpest focus on the closest eye and lips
- Soft circular bokeh in the foreground
- Fine 35mm film grain and subtle analog television texture
- Portrait aspect ratio: 2:3
