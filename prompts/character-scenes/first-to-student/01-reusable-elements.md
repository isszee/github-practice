# Reusable Elements

## Character direction — The Ice Queen

- Adult East Asian woman in her late twenties
- Precise, symmetrical features and porcelain skin
- Straight black jaw-length bob, usually immaculate
- Sharp dark eyes; frameless glasses as an authority cue
- Red lipstick as the only strong color accent
- Tailored black, white, cream, or navy wardrobe
- Controlled posture, precise gestures, and an intelligent gaze
- Emotional contrast: external competence versus private uncertainty

## Character direction — ENI

- Adult Thai woman in her late twenties
- Fair porcelain skin and long straight black hair
- Oval face, defined jawline, full pink lips, almond-shaped eyes
- Elegant, cinematic beauty with a conflicted or quietly expectant expression
- Wardrobe anchors: red silk robe, cream dress, white silk layers, red evening dress
- Use natural anatomy, realistic weight distribution, believable hands, and authentic posture

## Recurring environments

- Modern art gallery with white walls and abstract paintings
- Luxury condo with marble floors and minimalist interior
- High-end bathroom with marble surfaces and a backlit vanity mirror
- Fashion/editorial studio with black satin or dark neutral set pieces
- Late-night private interior with soft practical lights

## Lighting and color

- Soft diffused studio light for portrait work
- Warm golden-hour tones for elegant editorial scenes
- Cool moonlight balanced against warm practical light for night interiors
- High-contrast black and red for dramatic fashion studies
- Cream, ivory, navy, charcoal, and controlled red accents
- Realistic subsurface scattering, skin texture, fabric response, and physically motivated shadows

## Camera language

- Medium-format editorial look for polished portraits
- 35mm lens for candid gallery or environmental scenes
- 50mm lens for intimate cinematic portraits
- 85mm lens for fashion compression and elegant separation
- Smartphone mirror-selfie look for spontaneous private moments
- Shallow depth of field, natural optical falloff, fine film grain, and believable camera processing

## Emotional vocabulary

- Controlled
- Intelligent
- Conflicted
- Quietly expectant
- Elegant
- Vulnerable
- Secretive
- Cinematic

> Keep adult characters clearly adult and use implied intimacy rather than explicit anatomy when publishing to a public repository.
