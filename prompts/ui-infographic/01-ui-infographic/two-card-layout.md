# Two-Card Layout

Two side-by-side glassmorphism cards beneath a strong title section.

## Layout blocks

- Top: title and subtitle
- Center: two equal cards
- Bottom: divider and short footer

## Reusable prompt block

```text
Square professional UI infographic poster. Dark high-contrast background.
Large readable title at the top, short subtitle beneath it.
Two equal glassmorphism cards side-by-side with clear hierarchy, one icon per card,
short titles, and one-line descriptions. Thin luminous borders, consistent spacing,
clean alignment, restrained decorative effects, and highly legible typography.
```
