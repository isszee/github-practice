# Daily Info Card Template

## Use case

A compact daily information poster with a title, date, and a small number of content cards.

## Content schema

```yaml
infographic_type: Daily Info Card
main_title: "[MAIN TITLE]"
subtitle: "[DATE OR SHORT CONTEXT]"
cards:
  - number: 1
    icon: "[ICON DESCRIPTION]"
    title: "[CARD TITLE]"
    content: "[SHORT CONTENT]"
    badge_color: "[ACCENT COLOR]"
effects:
  - [EFFECT 1]
  - [EFFECT 2]
```

## Rules

- Use two or three cards unless the content truly needs more.
- Keep each card focused on one fact or idea.
- Use a short title and one concise content line.
- Keep the number of effects small enough that the text remains legible.
