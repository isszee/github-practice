# Three-Card Asymmetric Layout

A large feature card on the left with two smaller cards stacked on the right.

## Layout blocks

- Top: title, date, and optional opening line
- Center: one large vertical card on the left
- Center-right: two smaller rectangular cards
- Bottom: divider and short creative challenge

## Reusable prompt block

```text
Square professional UI/UX infographic poster with an asymmetric three-card composition:
one large vertical feature card on the left and two smaller cards stacked on the right.
Use a shared grid, aligned card edges, consistent margins, distinct but coordinated accents,
clear visual hierarchy, and enough negative space for readable text.
```
