# Color Palettes and Effects

## Effect families

- **Glow:** neon bloom around borders, icons, or key text.
- **Glass:** translucent cards with a soft blur and clear edge.
- **Scan:** restrained horizontal scan lines for a CRT feel.
- **Chromatic shift:** subtle color separation on selected edges only.
- **Reflection:** controlled highlights on chrome or liquid materials.
- **Particles:** sparse floating points that support depth.

## Selection rule

Choose one primary material effect, one lighting effect, and optionally one atmosphere effect. Too many simultaneous effects reduce clarity and make the infographic feel noisy.
