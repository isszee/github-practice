# UI Infographic Design System

## Structure

1. **Top section** — title, date, and short context line.
2. **Center section** — two-card or three-card content layout.
3. **Bottom section** — divider, label, and optional short challenge or takeaway.

## Card anatomy

- Number or small badge
- Icon
- Short title
- One-line description
- One accent color
- Optional material cue

## Typography

- Use one strong display style for the title.
- Use a simpler high-contrast face for card text.
- Avoid long paragraphs inside cards.
- Treat text legibility as a design requirement, not a decorative detail.

## Quality checklist

- Is the title readable at thumbnail size?
- Are card edges aligned?
- Is there enough contrast between text and background?
- Are effects supporting hierarchy rather than hiding it?
- Are the number of cards and effects limited?
