# Cyberpunk Neon

## Mood

Futuristic, energetic, high-contrast, digital nightlife.

## Palette

- Dark navy / midnight background
- Hot pink primary accent
- Electric cyan secondary accent

## Materials and effects

- Glassmorphism cards
- Neon glow
- Floating particles
- Scan lines
- Subtle chromatic aberration

## Prompt block

```text
Cyberpunk neon UI infographic, dark navy to midnight gradient, hot pink and electric cyan
accents, glassmorphism cards, controlled neon bloom, subtle CRT scan lines, restrained
chromatic aberration, crisp UI/UX grid, high contrast, readable typography, polished poster design.
```
